What was under test

PR #10118 (fix(etcd): make member reset and recovery idempotent) changes only etcd code: the rendered etcd StatefulSet asset, the shared/sharded reset-member script assembly in v2/etcd/shard.go, and the etcd-recovery Job logic. It does not touch the e2e suite, the kube-apiserver deployment, or any TLS-profile propagation. The verification therefore concentrated on (a) the reset-member decision matrix under mocked etcdctl failures, (b) the recovery Job's health classification and wait decisions, and (c) live recovery of a 3-member managed etcd on a hosted control plane created and reconciled by the PR-head control-plane operator image.
