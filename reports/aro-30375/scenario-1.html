Interpretation

This is the exact interrupted remove/add sequence from the ARO-30375 incident: the interruption happens between member remove and member add. The pre-PR behavior restarted the pod in bootstrap mode and recreated a duplicate identity. At head, the marker written before the interruption survives it, the retry completes the peer-URL add, and the pod never bootstraps a fresh cluster. The same property is exercised live in TC-007, where member removal and pod deletion are driven against the real 3-member etcd.
