TC-006 — Recovery waits for every healthy endpoint

PASS

Success may only be reported after every member endpoint is healthy: endpoint-only unhealthiness waits without deleting resources, and a repaired cluster is not declared healthy while any member lacks a client URL or any pod is still failing.
