The stock run-t1.sh patched mode encodes the CAPI 1.14 stall signature as its
success gate: physical rollout complete, upToDateReplicas == 0, status.version
advanced to the target, UpdatingVersion False, and config annotations changed.

On CAPI v1.13.4 the kubelet-version comparison that causes the 1.14 stall does
not exist, so upToDateReplicas reaches the desired count (3) six seconds after
the patch and stays there. The gate therefore cannot fire and the runner exits
1 at its 30-minute deadline even though the rollout completed normally at
2026-10-29T10:55:47Z.

verdict.json in this directory is the unmodified runner output and records
verdict=FAIL with expected.upToDateReplicasAtCompletion=0. It is retained as
raw evidence, not as the scenario verdict.

The verdict authority for this scenario is verify-t8.sh, whose output is in
verdict-t8.txt. It asserts the pre-1.14 expectations and exits non-zero on any
failure.
