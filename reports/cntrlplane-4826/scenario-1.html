The T1 runner samples the NodePool, MachineDeployment, MachineSets, Machines, guest Nodes and operator deployment every 5 s. The baseline and patched transitions above come from those samples, not from endpoint snapshots alone.

What this proves

  • The failure precondition is positive and measured: three Ready replacement Machines with upToDateReplicas=0, under CAPI v1.14.3.
  • The base operator freezes both an initial NodePool rollout and a release A→B Replace rollout at the same predicate.
  • Head completes an already-stuck rollout without any spec change, and completes a fresh A→B rollout from scratch.
  • Completion no longer depends on upToDateReplicas: that field was still 0 when head reported success in all runs.

What this does not prove

  • It does not exercise a pre-1.14 CAPI manager, a second infrastructure provider, or the InPlace upgrade path.
  • It does not rerun the config-only (T2), VM-size (T3) or scale-to-zero (T6) scenarios at head.
  • The PR's e2e Prow jobs were pending; only unit, verify, verify-deps, images and security are green at head.
