Harness note

run-t1.sh patched encodes the CAPI 1.14 stall signature as its success gate: it waits for upToDateReplicas == 0 with status.version advanced. On 1.13.4 the counter reaches 3, so that gate cannot fire and the stock runner runs to its deadline and then reports FAIL. The rollout itself completed normally at 2026-10-29T10:55:47Z; the archived trace is the primary evidence and verify-t8.sh is the verdict authority for this scenario. The runner was allowed to finish so its final snapshot and operator logs were archived. This limitation is recorded rather than hidden.
