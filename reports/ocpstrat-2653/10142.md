## Known limitation

On management clusters built from Kubernetes 1.30.3 or 1.31.2 (OpenShift 4.17 and
4.18), a HostedCluster created before this schema update with a now-disallowed
annotation can fail later status PATCH/UPDATE requests even when the annotation is
unchanged. The same scenario passed on 1.32.1 and later, and new-resource admission
passed across the whole matrix. This concerns pre-existing invalid configuration,
not the admission path this PR changes.
